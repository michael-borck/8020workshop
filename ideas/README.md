# Post ideas

One post a month. That is the whole plan.

This folder is a scratchpad, not a schedule you owe anyone. When the month comes around and the bench has given you nothing to say, pick a prompt from here and write 250 to 400 words. When the bench has given you something better, ignore this file and write that instead. Real beats planned, every time.

## The rules that keep this site itself

- Personal register only. Camping, astro, DIY, plain text, printers, and the thinking that connects them.
- The professional work can appear as a personal angle, once in a while, with no link and no pitch. The ten second check post from June 2026 is the limit. Do not go past it often.
- No funnel. No calls to action. No monetisation. No posting these to LinkedIn to feed the practice.
- Short is fine. Most posts here are under 400 words. A post can be one observation and one decision.
- Write two or three in one sitting when the mood is there. Date them a month apart. Let Hugo publish the past and present ones, and hold future ones as drafts until their month arrives.
- If a month passes with nothing, leave the gap. Backfill later only if the post is true to that month.

## The next six months

Dates are suggestions. Any day in the month works. Tags are from the existing set, so the homepage cloud needs no edits.

### November 2026 · the heat comes back

Working title. Too hot for the big scope.

Tags. astronomy, 80-20.

The season turns again. Write about packing the winter routine away and what summer observing asks for instead. Short nights, early starts, binoculars instead of the full setup. What did winter teach you that summer you would have skipped. A good post to write after the first warm night you gave up on and watched from a chair instead.

Stuck prompt. Name the last three sessions. What gear made it outside each time. What stayed in its case all winter.

### December 2026 · what left the workshop this year

Working title. Out the door.

Tags. marketplace, thinking.

A year end count, but keep it physical. What got sold, gifted, stripped, or binned under the one in, one out rule. What you miss, which is probably nothing, and what you are glad is gone. Photos of an empty shelf beat a list of resolutions. End on floor space, not on goals.

Stuck prompt. Walk the room with a notebook. Every item that left this year gets one line. The post is the lines with a short intro.

### January 2027 · one year of a trickle

Working title. A year of writing things down.

Tags. thinking, 80-20.

The first post on this site went up on 15 January 2026. A year on, what did the monthly habit actually do. Be honest about the rush at the start and the quieter months after it. Did writing things down change any decision at the bench or in the backyard. One concrete example is worth more than a reflection on consistency.

Stuck prompt. Reread your three favourite posts from the year. Say what each one got right that you had forgotten by the time you wrote it down.

### February 2027 · the bench in summer

Working title. Hot workshop, slow projects.

Tags. printing, restomod.

Perth summer slows the workshop down too. Heat in the room, prints warping, jobs you put off until autumn. Write about the one small job that suits a hot month. Sorting parts, labelling boxes, updating a config, testing one printer while the others rest. Small jobs done in summer are what autumn builds stand on.

Stuck prompt. Pick the ugliest box on the shelf. Sort it. Photograph the before and after. Write what you found.

### March 2027 · first dark trip of the year

Working title. Back under a dark sky.

Tags. astronomy, nomad.

Autumn is first trip season. Even if the trip is short, write the plan before you go and add two lines after. What made the list this time, what got left at home after the July 2026 post, and whether the lighter kit held up. Practical details help a reader plan their own trip. Drive time, water, warmth, setup time.

Stuck prompt. No trip happened. Write the backyard version instead. One clear night, one scope, what you saw, when you packed up and why.

### April 2027 · the Amalgam, one honest paragraph at a time

Working title. Where the Amalgam actually is.

Tags. printing, amalgam.

A plain status post. What works, what is stuck, what changed since the intro post in January 2026, and what you would do first with one free weekend. Honest beats impressive. A stuck project written up clearly is more useful to the next builder than a polished launch post. Link the project site once, since this post is about that project, and leave it there.

Stuck prompt. Answer three questions in a notebook first. What printed last. What broke last. What is the smallest next step. The post writes itself from there.

## Backlog, for any month

Pick by mood, not by order.

### Astro and camping

- The six inch Newt test. One night, one modest scope, a list of what it actually showed from a suburban backyard.
- Red torch economics. The cheap gear you reach for every session and the pricey gear still in its box.
- A chair is astronomy equipment. Comfort as the difference between ten minutes and two hours outside.
- Notes from a cloudy trip. What a washed out dark sky trip is still good for.
- The neighbour light problem, solved with a fence, a hood, and a conversation.

### Printers and DIY

- The box of cables. Every workshop has one. Sort it, label it, throw half of it out, write it up.
- Listening to a printer. What a healthy machine sounds like and the noise that means stop now.
- One tool, sharpened. Or calibrated, or cleaned. Maintenance as a post.
- The part I printed twice. A failed first version, the fix, and what changed between them.
- Filament maths. What a roll really costs when you count failures and test prints.

### Plain text and home coding

- The notebook on the bench. Paper notes during a build, typed up later or not at all.
- A script that saves ten minutes a month. Small automation at home, written once.
- Backups for boring files. Where the text files live, and the day you will be glad.
- The folder tour. One project folder, explained. Readers like seeing how other people organise work.
- Deleting an app. Something you stopped using and what plain text replaced it with.

### Thinking

- Buying time, not things. A Marketplace purchase judged by the hours it gave back.
- The project I am not starting. Saying no to a tempting build, and what filled the space.
- Good enough, out loud. A job you stopped at 80 percent and have not regretted.
- Learning in public, one year on. What posting the failures cost and what it returned.
- Seasons of attention. The hyperfocus cycle across a full year, now that you have the record to check.

## A 20 minute routine for a stuck month

1. Look at the last ten photos on your phone from the workshop or the backyard.
2. Pick the one with a problem in it. Broken, dusty, half built, cloudy. Problems write easier than successes.
3. Write what happened in five plain sentences. Then one sentence on what you did. Then one on what you would do next time.
4. Check it against the rules at the top of this file. Cut any sentence that sounds like the practice.
5. Set the tags, set the date, build the site, read it once rendered, then push.

That is a post. Some months it is the whole post, and that is fine.
