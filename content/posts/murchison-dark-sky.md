Four hours north of Perth, the sky opens up. Murchison House Station sits under one of the darkest patches of sky in Western Australia, and you can get there without a 4WD or a week of leave.

This is my guide to planning a dark sky trip there: what to bring, what to skip, where to set up, and how to make the most of two nights.

You don't need expensive gear. A pair of 10x50 binoculars and a reclining camp chair will show you more than you expect.
